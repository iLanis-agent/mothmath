Moth math

One moth on the sweater means larvae in the wardrobe, and the advice online is a fog of cedar balls and lavender. Moth math turns the response into numbers: how many pheromone traps the wardrobes take and what a year of replacements costs, how many freezer loads the woolens need and how many days that runs end to end, and what the hot wash costs against the dry-clean bill for the same pile. Exact counting, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Trap plan - wardrobes become traps out now, bought per year and a yearly cost.
  • Freeze plan - the pile becomes freezer loads and days end to end.
  • Wash plan - washer loads against the dry-clean bill for the same items.

Labeled norms

Labeled trap life: 90 days, one trap per wardrobe. Labeled freeze: 72 hours at -18 C per load, 6 items to a load. Labeled wash: $1.50 a load against $12 an item dry-cleaned. These are reference values from common pest-care guidance, not guarantees - delicates, heavy infestations and treasured pieces deserve a professional's call.
